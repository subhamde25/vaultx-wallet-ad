# Style research (2026-11-11)

Sources looked at: prompt-motion.com (Phone / Product UI filters), originkit.dev trending (Fintra and other
fintech Framer templates), templens.com search "dark crypto wallet fintech app landing page with green accent"
(CryptoVault, VAULT 27 "Wealth, sealed.", Cryptix, NexaCoin, "Crypto moves fast. Stay in control." dashboard,
Quantia "The Financial OS"), YouTube search "crypto wallet app promo motion graphics after effects"
(Envato crypto-wallet promo templates, Apple-style wallet UI animation tutorials, finance app UI promos).

Patterns worth borrowing (ideas only, nothing copied):
- Near-black canvas with one soft radial green glow, faint dot grid; one lime accent, everything else white/grey.
- Huge balance numbers in a tight grotesk, small grey labels, pill buttons in the accent colour.
- Phone mockup as the anchor; feature cards pop out of the phone and float beside it with depth.
- Green bar and line charts that draw on; a dot rides the line tip with a value tag.
- Glassy cards (subtle 1 px border, slightly lighter fill), rounded 24-32 px corners.
- Headline words reveal from a mask line; camera pushes and tilts between beats rather than hard cuts.
- Security beats: shield outline draws, lock closes, fingerprint ridges trace on, scan line sweeps.

Our system: bg #0E110E -> #171B17, card #1E231E / border #2C332C, brand charcoal #3E433E,
accent #91C950 (glow, pills, chart), red #E5484D only for a negative ticker, white #FFFFFF text,
grey #8A938A labels. Type: Inter / Inter Display (Bold, SemiBold, Medium). 1920x1080, 30 fps.
