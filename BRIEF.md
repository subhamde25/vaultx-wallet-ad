# Brief

**Client test:** motion design assignment ("Task 5"), received 2026-11-11. This is a summary of the brief,
not the original text.

**Task:** a 45-second motion graphics ad for a crypto wallet, **VaultX Wallet**, animated to a supplied AI
voiceover. It should feel like a professional fintech product launch and a real product ad, not a slideshow
of graphics. No reference video was given, so the creative direction is open.

## Supplied

A brand kit with the script, the voiceover and the logo, plus the company name, VaultX Wallet.

## Required visual elements

- Logo or product name animation (intro or outro)
- At least one animated data visualisation (line or bar chart)
- Feature callout animations
- A security visual (lock, shield, encryption)
- A strong end screen with a call to action

## Design and animation

- Clean layout and spacing, a strong type hierarchy
- Smooth easing, no linear keyframes
- Intentional transitions, no random presets
- Pacing that follows the voiceover, with kinetic type where it fits
- Simplicity and polish over flashy effects

## Technical

- 1920×1080 (16:9)
- Clean masking and alignment, motion blur where it fits
- An organised project file with clearly labelled comps and layers
- No heavy reliance on templates; any music the editor chooses

## To deliver

- The final video, H.264, high quality
- The project file, collected and organised
- Any custom assets made for it (icons, UI screens and so on)

## Choices made on top of the brief

- Built in **DaVinci Resolve's Fusion page**, at the author's choice.
- No sound effect used more than twice.
- The logo is used exactly as supplied, although its wordmark reads "Valut.X" while the script and
  voiceover say "VaultX".
- The script line "See where your money is going — and where it's growing." is not in the voiceover, so it
  has no scene.
- All numbers, balances and charts are illustrative.
